THE RESET ROOM

This space is for moments when your habits, attention or emotions feel difficult to manage. You can acknowledge a difficult moment without judging yourself.

When you're struggling

1. Name the situation.
What is happening? What happened just before this moment?

2. Change your environment.
Move to a different activity or a shared space. Put away distractions.

3. Choose a replacement.
Read, draw, learn, walk, or speak with someone you trust.

4. Review your decision.
What could make the next difficult moment easier?

Quick reflection

RESET is an educational support tool, not a medical service. If you feel overwhelmed or persistently low, speak with a trusted adult, school counsellor or qualified health professional.
