When things get difficult

RESET Room

A practical pause. No judgement, no dramatic speeches. Focus on the next safe decision.

Take a 60-second reset

Put some distance between yourself and the situation. Breathe slowly, notice where you are, and choose what to do next.

60

Ready when you are.

Ask yourself

1. What is happening?

Name the situation without insulting yourself.

2. What might be influencing me?

Consider boredom, stress, loneliness, tiredness, or a familiar routine.

3. What is one better next step?

Change your environment, contact someone you trust, or begin a useful activity.
