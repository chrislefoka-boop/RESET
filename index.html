01 / Awareness

Understand your patterns.

Habits can connect to situations, feelings, and routines. Noticing the pattern gives you more choices.

Common triggers

Stress

Some habits become a quick way to escape uncomfortable feelings.

Boredom

Unstructured time can make automatic routines easier to repeat.

Loneliness

Connection and support can make difficult moments easier.

Environment

Places, devices, and repeated cues can prompt familiar behaviour.

Reflect privately

What usually happens just before the habit you want to change?

02 / Make space

Pause. Breathe. Choose.

An urge can feel intense, but you can give yourself time before deciding what to do next.

Five-minute reset
05:00
Try changing rooms, getting water, stretching gently, or messaging someone you trust.

Choose one small action

• Step away for a moment.
• Put your phone down and notice your surroundings.
• Do a simple task that uses your attention.
• Talk to a trusted person.

03 / Learn

Build useful skills.

Short lessons to help you understand change.

01 — How habits work

A cue can lead to a repeated action. Changing the cue or choosing a different response can reshape a routine.

02 — Difficult moments

Name what you are feeling without judgement, then choose one safe activity while the feeling changes.

03 — Design your environment

Make helpful choices easier to reach. Reduce reminders of routines you are trying to change.

04 — Respond to setbacks

A setback is information, not a definition of who you are. Review what happened and choose a next step.
